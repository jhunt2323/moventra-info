Privacy Policy

Moventra: Home Workouts, by Joseph Huntley / capableprocess.ai. Effective November 10, 2026.

The short version

Moventra does not collect, sell or share any personal information. There are no ads, analytics or tracking, and the app has no server.

What stays on your phone

Your profile (goals, sport, fitness level, limitations, workout days), your workouts, levels, records, badges and any weight you log are saved only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Sign in with Apple

Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Apple, Apple gives the app an anonymous account ID and, the first time only, the name and email you choose to share. Moventra keeps these on your iPhone only and does not send them anywhere. You can sign out in Settings at any time.

Reminders

If you turn on workout reminders, they are scheduled on your iPhone by iOS. Nothing is sent to us.

Health

Moventra is a general fitness app, not medical advice. It does not read or write Apple Health data.

Children

Moventra does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: info@capableprocess.ai.
