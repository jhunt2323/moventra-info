How do I change how long today's workout is?

On the Today tab, tap 5, 10, 15, 20, 30 or 45 minutes, or Other for any time from 3 to 60 minutes. The workout rebuilds to fit.

How does the app decide what's next?

Workouts rotate between upper body + core, legs + cardio, and full body (or your sport day if you picked a sport). Days you didn't pick are light stretch days, and after three hard days in a row you get a recovery day.

How do moves get harder?

After each workout, rate each move Too easy, Just right or Too hard. Targets grow as you rate them, and past the top of a range you move up to a harder version. Too hard, or the Easier button, steps you back.

Can I change my goals, sport or limitations?

Yes. Tap the gear on the Today tab to open Settings.

Is it safe?

Moventra isn't medical advice. Stop if something hurts, and check with a doctor before starting a new exercise program.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
